Copying a signature with its logo

Press Copy signature under any signature, or select it with the mouse and press Ctrl+C, then paste into the signature box of your mail app. The whole signature is copied as clean HTML, and it always carries a logo the mail app can fetch.

To paste the animated logo everywhere: upload assets/innotech-diamond-logo-animated.gif from the package to the website's media library, then paste the file's address here. Until then, pasted signatures use the static logo that is already on innotechdiamond.com.

The same template for both people; only their details change. A desktop mail window on the left, a 360 px phone on the right. The logo turns once every four seconds; its first frame is the complete logo, which is what a mail app shows if it does not play animation.

Three design concepts

Signature generator

For any employee: fill in the details, then press Copy signature and paste into the mail app's signature settings. A field left empty shows as a placeholder. Department is optional.
